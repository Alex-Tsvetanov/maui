> [!TIP]
> Placing a [[CollectionView|CollectionView]] inside a [[VerticalStackLayout|VerticalStackLayout]] or [[StackLayout (Controls)|StackLayout]] can prevent the [[CollectionView|CollectionView]] from scrolling, can prevent the `ScrollTo%2A` method from scrolling the [[CollectionView|CollectionView]], and can limit the number of items that are displayed. In this situation, replace the [[VerticalStackLayout|VerticalStackLayout]] or [[StackLayout (Controls)|StackLayout]] with a [[Grid (Controls)|Grid]].
