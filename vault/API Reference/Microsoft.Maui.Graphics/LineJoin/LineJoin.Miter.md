# LineJoin.Miter

> [!abstract] Field of [[LineJoin|LineJoin]]
> Namespace: `Microsoft.Maui.Graphics`

Creates a sharp corner or point. The outer edges of the lines extend until they meet at a point. If the joint's angle is too sharp, a bevel join is used instead.
